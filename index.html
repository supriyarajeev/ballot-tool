How this guide works. Facts come from official sources first: the California Secretary of State, the Legislative Analyst's Office, county elections offices and official measure documents. Each item shows its status and the date it was last checked. Nothing is marked Verified until a person has reviewed it against the official source. Campaign arguments are attributed to the side that makes them. The guide does not recommend how to vote, and it does not score or rank candidates. The statewide Standard Evaluation Framework explains how proposition evidence scores work. Scores are decision aids, not validated predictions, and missing evidence is never treated as proof that a measure will fail or succeed.

Privacy. You never need to enter an address, name or contact details. Your county and city or town choices stay in the page address after the # sign. This site does not save them or send them anywhere, but like any web page your browser history keeps the address and the page title. Your answers in the statewide proposition tool are saved only in this browser. The pages load fonts from Google Fonts and a visitor counter from GoatCounter, which receive standard request details such as your IP address. GoatCounter counts one visit each time a page loads, using only that page's fixed name and title, plus the referring site, browser, screen size, language and country. In the proposition tool it also counts whether someone answered at least one question and all 14. It is never sent the part of the address after the #, so it does not receive your county, city or town, the items you open, your answers or your results.

Election information can change. Always confirm with your county's official sample ballot and the official California voter guide (opens in a new tab).
