# Candidate audit

_Generated by `tools/report.mjs` from the files in `data/`. Do not edit by hand; edit the data and rerun._

71 contests and 190 candidates. Status counts: contests Needs review 71; candidates Needs review 190.

This guide never scores, ranks, endorses or recommends candidates. They appear in the order of the official source. Only official facts are recorded:
- name;
- party preference, for partisan offices only;
- ballot designation, where the source gives one;
- incumbency, where the source marks it;
- where to find the candidate statement.

## Candidate order

`tools/validate.mjs` compares the order of every contest with `tests/fixtures/candidate-order.json`, recorded on 2026-10-23 for 71 contests. A change in order fails validation until the fixture is re-recorded on purpose. The fixture records the order as entered from the official source; it does not by itself prove that order is right.

## Checks done on October 22, 2026 (hand-written audit record; counts computed from data)

- **Statewide and Board of Equalization (12 contests now in data).** The certified list (Aug 27, 2026) was read three times.
  - Names, parties, designations and order were compared with the list.
  - Incumbent marks follow the list's asterisks.
  - The "no statement" notes were confirmed on the state guide.
- **Congressional and legislative (14 contests now in data).** Compared with the certified list.
  - The county election pages were used to confirm which districts appear on each county's ballots.
- **San Francisco local (11 contests now in data).**
  - Names, order and seat counts were compared with the Department of Elections page.
  - Statement page numbers come from the Voter Information Pamphlet.
  - No official source showing ballot designations was found.
- **Oakland, Berkeley and Hayward.** These match the city clerk documents.
  - On October 22 Berkeley designations were left out, because the pairing came from line order. On October 23 they were added from the city's Aug 19 notice of nominees, read visually, where each name is directly followed by its designation. Where the notice prints no designation, none is recorded.
- **Added on October 23, 2026.** The Berkeley Rent Stabilization Board, from the City Clerk's final roster.
- **New contests from official city and district sources:**
  - City of Alameda, Albany (council and school board), Emeryville, Piedmont, San Leandro and Union City;
  - EBMUD Wards 3 and 7.

## Left out on purpose

- **Oakland Unified board districts.** Left out on October 22. On October 23 three district contests were added from a visual reading of the Oakland City Clerk's candidate list, where each district heading sits above its names.
- **Newark.** Only a filing-activity notice was found, not a final roster.
- **Pleasanton.** The list does not say which office each name seeks.
- **Oakland write-in for Mayor.** Mentioned in the contest notes, but not listed as a ballot candidate.

## Still missing

- **Alameda County (hand-written, Oct 22, 2026).** No county-wide roster could be read. These were missing:
  - Fremont, Dublin, Livermore, Newark and Pleasanton;
  - AC Transit, BART Districts 2, 4 and 6;
  - the Peralta and Chabot-Las Positas college districts, and other school districts;
- **Other counties (computed).** Contra Costa, Marin, Napa, San Mateo, Santa Clara, Solano and Sonoma have local contests marked not collected.
- **Candidate details.** Candidate qualifications, positions and public records have not been collected. They will only be added from official or clearly attributed sources.
