**What `tools/validate.mjs` enforces:**
- every published field (the field list is shared with `tools/build-ledger.mjs`) has an evidence record;
- every claim-level record has a non-empty excerpt, an explanation, a verdict, a retrieval date that is a real date and not in the future, and a capture method;
- a claim-level record names a field the record actually publishes;
- a verdict of "unsupported" or "not-found" never backs a published field;
- every published threshold, ballot question and measure type has claim-level evidence with a non-empty excerpt;
- a claim whose every source is unavailable or not text-extracted is reported as a warning;
- record-level links never carry excerpts or a verified status;
- a verified record has claim-level evidence for every published field;
- `data/evidence.json` matches `data/evidence-claims.json` (a stale ledger fails).

**What it does not check.** Whether an excerpt is relevant to its claim, and whether the excerpt appears word for word in the source, are not machine-checked. A person must compare them.
