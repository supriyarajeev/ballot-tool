# Candidate photo manifest (October 24, 2026, updated for the owner's photo decision)

**Rule.** Owner decision for the beta release (Oct 24, 2026). A photo is shown for each candidate whose photo (1) is tagged on Wikimedia Commons as public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA, including PD-CAGov photos of Legislature members whose legal basis is not settled, and (2) was matched to the candidate by more than name (Wikidata item or file description naming the office). Photos are no longer hidden when an opponent has none; that candidate gets a neutral placeholder and the page explains it. Official voter guide photos are not reused because no license is given. A file whose license looked doubtful (Gloria Romero) is not used.

**Search.** All 190 candidates were searched on Wikidata/Wikimedia Commons on Oct 24, 2026 through the Claude desktop browser. A search that finds nothing does not mean no photo exists.

**License check.** Licenses were read from each file's Wikimedia Commons record (license, artist, credit and license templates) through the Commons API in the Claude desktop browser on Oct 24, 2026. An independent reviewer could not open those pages from its tools, so this reading has not been repeated by a second check. The 26 photos added on the owner's decision had their Commons license templates, artist, date and description read through the Commons API in the Claude desktop browser on Oct 24, 2026.

**Legal note.** Wikimedia Commons' PD-CAGov reading rests on the California Public Records Act, which does not cover the Legislature. Photos credited to the State Assembly or Senate (Shirley N. Weber, Donald P. (Don) Wagner, Ben Allen, Shannon Grove, Scott Wiener, Buffy Wicks, Rebecca Bauer-Kahan, Mia Bonta, Catherine Stefani, Liz Ortega, Alex Lee) therefore have no settled legal basis. The site shows them on the owner's decision, with credits, a disclaimer, and a removal route through the GitHub repository. This is not a legal opinion.
