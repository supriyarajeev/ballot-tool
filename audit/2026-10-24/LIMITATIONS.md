# Known limitations (October 24, 2026 release)

Each item is also disclosed on the site where it matters.

## Data

1. **No human review.** No record has been checked by a person against an official source or a sample ballot. Nothing is marked Verified.
2. **Local contests.** 412 local contests are listed across the nine counties (some cross-county contests counted twice; COVERAGE-MATRIX.md). They come from county lists read by automated research or by the project lead in a browser. Two Sonoma fire district contests are pending, from a filing list only.
3. **Candidate names.** Some counties print names in capitals; the capitalization is a best guess.
4. **Candidate details.** Statements, qualifications, positions and public records are not collected for most local candidates. Statement links are mostly missing.
5. **Qualified write-in candidates** are not listed (for example Hina Patel, Orchard School District; Jacob Montoya, Morgan Hill Mayor; Reichi Lee, Berkeley Unified).
6. **Seats not on the ballot** are listed per county from the county lists. The reason (appointment in lieu of election) is inferred from "not on ballot" or "off ballot" labels where a county does not state it.
7. **Local measures** outside Alameda are listed by letter and title only.
8. **Districts.** School and special district boundaries are not recorded, so "May apply" labels cannot be narrowed to an address.
9. **Registration figures** are from the 60-day report (September 4, 2026). The 15-day report was not published when checked.

## Ratings and scoring

10. **Proposition ratings** are structured judgments made with AI research assistance under one rubric. They are not official and no outside person has reviewed them.
11. **Candidates are not rated.** Candidate evaluation mode shows Insufficient evidence for every factor.

## Photos

12. **Coverage is 2.5%** (31 of 1,225 candidates), far below the 80% target. The target could not be met legitimately: most local candidates have no image with a documented reuse basis, and the official voter guide photos carry no license.
13. **Eight photos rely on Commons' PD-CAGov reading** for state and local government works, which is not a court ruling on those photos.

## Technical

14. The site needs JavaScript. Without it, only a short notice shows.
15. Google Fonts and the GoatCounter visitor counter are loaded from outside; if blocked, the site still works with system fonts and without counting.
16. Tests run in Chromium only. Safari and Firefox were not tested in this release.
17. Nothing was deployed, so no test ran against the live site.
